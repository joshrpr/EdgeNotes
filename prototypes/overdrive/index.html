Three ways to overdrive Edge Notes

The same knife page, the same data, three different worlds. Each keeps the bench-first layout and the one-thumb flow; they change what the page is made of and what happens when you save. The library would take the same material in each direction.

Kuro-uchi

The blade becomes the page. A drawn kurouchi gyuto carries the last angle, the certificate reads like an engraved tag, and the library becomes a magnetic rack the knife slides out of.

The stone

The whetstone becomes the instrument. The knife page is a wet stone with the blade resting at its angle; the log sheet raises the blade as the angle is typed.

Feel
Typing 21° lifts the blade with spring resistance, the included angle opens live, and a save leaves a bright burr and a water bead.
Cost
A real interaction build: JS springs on the log sheet, but no heavy rendering. The stone has to feel wet, not grey.

The forge

The knife page is the quench. A red-hot blade cools to steel above the trough; steam and sparks carry the save, and the library holds the same heat.

Feel
The edge glows, the steam drifts, and a save throws one burst of sparks. Immediate and cinematic.
Cost
Highest motion budget: canvas or SVG particles and a reduced-motion still. The easiest of the three to overdo.
